CUSTOM PRODUCTION / PRIVATE LABEL

Your idea.
Developed with intent.

For brands that need something specific — from a reference sample to a detailed product brief — custom development starts by defining the product before moving into the details.

MATERIAL / COLOUR / CONSTRUCTION

FROM CONCEPT TO SPECIFICATION

Build a product that feels like your brand.

Custom production can involve many decisions. We help frame the conversation around the product, the intended customer and the technical details that will define the result.

Depending on the project and available production route, discussions may cover material selection, fit, measurements, trims, labels, prints, embroidery, packaging and quality expectations. Development steps, sampling availability, minimum quantities and timelines must be confirmed before a project is committed.

WHAT CAN BE DEVELOPED?

Details that shape
the final product.

The following are common areas to define during a custom product conversation. Not every option applies to every category or production partner.

01 / APPAREL

Silhouette & fit

Garment measurements, grading, fit intent, construction details and finishing.

02 / MATERIALS

Fabric & hand-feel

Fibre content, weight, texture, colour, performance and care requirements.

03 / ACCESSORIES

Components & finish

Hardware, trims, lining, edge finish, closures and functional details.

04 / BRANDING

Labels & identity

Prints, embroidery, woven labels, hangtags, packaging and brand placement.

A TYPICAL DEVELOPMENT FLOW

Move forward in clear stages.

Every project differs. This framework shows the conversations that may be needed before an order is confirmed.

01

Brief & references

Share a tech pack, sketches, photos, sample or written product requirements.

02

Feasibility & costing discussion

Clarify target price, materials, quantity, construction and any technical constraints.

03

Sampling & review

If available and agreed, review a sample, record feedback and confirm revisions.

04

Order alignment

Confirm approved specifications, quantities, quality checkpoints, packing and schedule.
