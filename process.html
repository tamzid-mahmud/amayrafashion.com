SPECIFICATIONS BEFORE ASSUMPTIONS

QUALITY STARTS WITH AGREEMENT

Define quality before the order. Not after.

“Good quality” means different things to different buyers. The best time to discuss it is before the product moves forward.

01

Material and construction

Fibre content, weight, components, stitching, finishing and durability needs.

02

Measurements and tolerances

Size charts, fit references, grading and acceptable variation where applicable.

03

Inspection and packing

Agreed checks, defect definitions, labels, carton requirements and documentation.

A FEW USEFUL QUESTIONS

Before we start working together.

Do you work with ready-made and custom products?

Yes, the website presents both as enquiry routes. The suitable route, product availability, production access and terms need to be confirmed for each project.

Can you work from a sample or tech pack?

A sample, tech pack, sketch or reference image is useful for a custom brief. We will review the information provided and clarify what else is needed.

Can you confirm minimum order quantities and lead times?

These vary by product, material, production route and supplier. They should be confirmed in writing before a buyer commits to an order.

Do you offer certifications or factory audits?

Any certifications, audit reports or compliance documentation must be confirmed for the specific facility or supplier. We do not claim blanket certifications on this prototype site.
